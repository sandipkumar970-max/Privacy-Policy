1. Introduction & Core Privacy Principles

Muslim One is developed as a sincere, enduring digital service (Sadaqah Jariyah) for Muslims worldwide. We believe that your worship, spiritual reflections, and personal religious practices deserve privacy.

  • Ad-Free Experience: Muslim One is intended to operate without advertisements, banners, pop-ups, or affiliate promotions.
  • No User Accounts: The app does not require account registration, email sign-in, phone number, or social login.
  • No Behavioral Profiling: We do not intentionally collect or sell your reading habits, prayer frequency, or usage patterns for advertising or commercial profiling.
  • Local-First Design: Core utilities, including Quran text available offline, prayer-time calculations, Qibla compass, Tasbih, Namaz guide, and Zakat calculator, are designed to work locally where supported by the installed app.

2. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

Muslim One is designed to minimize personal data collection. We do not operate a developer-managed user database for the local information described in this policy.

A. Location Data

Permissions: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION.

Purpose:

  • Calculate local prayer times using geographic coordinates.
  • Calculate the Qibla direction toward the Kaaba in Mecca.
  • Find nearby mosques when you explicitly use Mosque Finder.

How Location Is Handled:

  • Prayer-time and Qibla calculations are designed to run on-device. Coordinates are not sent to Muslim One developer servers for these calculations.
  • Mosque Finder may send coordinates and a search radius over HTTPS to the OpenStreetMap Overpass API at overpass-api.de. Map tiles may be requested from tile.openstreetmap.org. These third-party services may process technical connection data, including IP addresses.
  • Where implemented, search results may be cached locally to reduce repeated network requests.
  • Location permission is optional. If supported by the installed version, you can manually select a city instead.

B. Device Sensors

The Qibla compass may use the device's magnetometer and accelerometer to determine direction. Sensor readings are intended to be processed locally and are not intentionally transmitted to developer servers.

C. Audio & Storage

Quran recitations may be streamed or downloaded from EveryAyah.com over HTTPS. Downloaded audio is intended to be stored in private app storage and is not intentionally uploaded by Muslim One.

D. User-Generated Data

Bookmarks, reflection notes, Khatam Quran goals, Tasbih/Dhikr counts, prayer logs, fasting logs, and Zakat calculations are intended to be stored locally using the app's database and Android DataStore, where those features are implemented. Muslim One does not provide a developer-managed remote account database for these records.

3. Cloud Backups & Google Account Storage

If Android backup is enabled and the app's backup configuration permits it, some app data may be included in Android or Google backup services. Actual backup behavior depends on the app configuration, device, Android version, and account settings.

  • Google handles backups according to its applicable policies and settings.
  • Backup encryption and retention depend on the relevant Android and Google backup implementation. End-to-end encryption is not guaranteed for every backup.
  • Databases, downloaded audio, and cache files are excluded only where the app's backup rules actually configure those exclusions.
  • The developer does not have direct access to your Google account or personal backup archives.

6. Children's Privacy

Muslim One is intended for a general audience and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-managed account system. The app's actual compliance with COPPA, GDPR-K, and other applicable laws depends on its implementation, audience, and operation.

7. Data Retention, Control, and Deletion

  • Clear App Data: You can use Android Settings > Apps > Muslim One > Storage & cache > Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Unsynced local information may be permanently lost.
  • Selective Deletion: Where available, individual bookmarks, notes, audio downloads, and Tasbih records may be deleted within their respective app screens.
  • Third-Party Records: Third-party service providers may retain network request logs under their own policies. Contact the relevant provider for questions about those records.

8. Security

  • Supported network communications use HTTPS/TLS where available.
  • Private app storage benefits from Android's application sandbox.
  • The app is intended to avoid advertising frameworks and behavioral tracking analytics. Actual dependencies and network behavior should be reviewed against the released build.
