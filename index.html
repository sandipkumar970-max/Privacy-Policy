This Privacy Policy explains how Muslim One ("the App"), developed by EllistApp, handles information when you use the App.

1. Introduction

Muslim One is a free Islamic application offering features such as prayer times, Azan, Qibla direction, Quran, Dua, Tasbih and related Islamic tools. The App is designed to work mainly on your device without requiring an account, advertisements or subscriptions, where supported by the released version.

2. Our Privacy Commitments

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • The App does not require account creation, if this matches its implementation.
  • We do not display advertisements, if no advertising SDK is integrated.
  • We do not use advertising or behavioural-tracking SDKs, if accurate for the released version.
  • Information saved locally remains on your device unless a feature or third-party service transmits it.

3. Location Information

The App may request approximate or precise location permission to calculate prayer times, sunrise and sunset, Sehri and Iftar timings, Qibla direction or local timezone.

Where implemented, location calculations occur on your device. You may deny location permission and select a city manually if that option is available. Any use of location by Google Play services or another provider is subject to that provider's privacy practices.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

Depending on the features you use, the App may store settings, selected city, bookmarks, notes, reading progress, prayer tracking, Qaza records, fasting trackers, Tasbih counts, favourites, Zakat calculation history, Hifz progress and downloaded audio.

Data stored locally can generally be removed by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the App. This does not necessarily delete information held by third parties.

5. Device Sensors

If the Qibla compass feature is available, the App may use motion and magnetic sensors to calculate direction. Sensor readings are processed on your device where implemented that way.

6. Notifications and Alarms

The App may use Android notifications and alarms to provide Azan notifications and prayer reminders. The exact permissions and behaviour depend on the App's implementation and Android version.

7. Internet and Third-Party Services

Some optional features may require internet access. Depending on the services integrated into the App, third parties may receive technical information such as your IP address and requested resources.

Possible service categories include:

  • Quran audio streaming or download providers.
  • Map services or Mosque Finder providers.
  • City lookup and reverse-geocoding services.
  • Google Play services for location functionality.
  • Gold or silver price services, if used for Zakat calculations.

This list must be updated to name the actual providers used by the released App. Remove services that are not integrated. Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies.

8. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

If accurate for the released version, we do not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone recordings, camera data or advertising ID. Any collection by third-party SDKs must be disclosed accurately.

9. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising purposes. Technical information may be transmitted to service providers when you use features that depend on them.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

You can generally delete locally stored data by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the App. Downloaded content may also be removable within the App. Information held by third-party providers is subject to their retention and deletion practices.

11. Children's Privacy

Muslim One provides general Islamic information. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information directly. The App's target audience and data practices must comply with applicable law and Google Play policies.

12. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the App. Android's application sandbox may help protect locally stored data. Network connections should use HTTPS where supported. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

13. Religious Content Disclaimer

Prayer times and Qibla direction may vary because of location accuracy, calculation methods and local religious authorities. Please confirm important timings with a trusted local mosque or authority. Zakat calculations are estimates and should not replace qualified advice.

14. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of personal information. Contact us below with privacy-related questions.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. We will revise the effective date when we publish an updated version.
